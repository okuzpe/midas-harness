# Midas harness — product idea (engine dogfood)

**Product:** midas-harness — a portable product-development methodology kit for AI coding agents.

**One-liner:** Drive a software product from idea to shipped code through 9 audited phases, with
cost-tiered agents, CHECK-backed rules, and a deterministic installer.

**Users:** Teams and solo builders who want agent-governed delivery (not a convention-only rules dump).

**Out of scope for this meta-product:** end-user SaaS features, hosted cloud of Midas itself.
